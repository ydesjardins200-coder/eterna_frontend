Good to know

Why plan for something I hope I never need?

Precisely because it is unexpected. Deciding while you are well means the decision is made calmly — not under pressure, and not by someone else in grief. It is a kindness you extend forward.

What makes the Capsule Pyrale different?

A hermetic aluminium capsule that preserves naturally, with nitrogen, for sixty to a hundred years — without chemicals or embalming. Designed and made in Québec; each one carries its own serial number.

Can I change my mind later?

Yes. You can revisit the details at any time, and your cancellation rights are preserved. One call to your advisor is all it takes.

How is my money protected?

Prepaid prearrangements in Québec are safeguarded under provincial regulation. Before anything is finalized, your advisor explains in writing exactly how your funds are held.

What if I move, or my family lives in another province?

Your plan stays on file with us. We coordinate with a funeral home wherever the service takes place, and your advisor walks you through what changes, if anything.
