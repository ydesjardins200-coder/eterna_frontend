The process

How it works

A seamless, controlled process that combines engineering precision with the deepest respect for life.

01

Hermetic Sealing

The cover is lowered and locked with a special key, creating a completely airtight barrier — no leaks, no odors, no infiltration possible.

02

Inert Gas Preservation

The capsule is filled with nitrogen gas, naturally drying the body without chemicals or toxic release. Preservation lasts up to 100 years.

03

Gentle Placement

The body is placed inside by qualified professionals. Every gesture is slow, deliberate, and respectful — no rushed movements, no compromise on dignity.

Once the Capsule Pyrale is hermetically sealed, it is filled with inert nitrogen gas. Nitrogen displaces the oxygen inside the capsule, eliminating the conditions that allow decomposition to occur. The gas naturally dries the body over time — a process that is entirely physical, with no chemical agents involved. The result is preservation of up to 60 to 100 years.

Yes. The capsule is built from 100% recyclable aluminium and releases no toxins into the soil or groundwater. There are no embalming chemicals, making it one of the most responsible interment options available.

Because the capsule is hermetically sealed with no fluid or odor, it is fully compliant with international air-cargo regulations and certified for transport in Canada and the USA.
